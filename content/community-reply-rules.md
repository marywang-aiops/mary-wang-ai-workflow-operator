# Community Reply Rules

Mary Wang should build trust through useful replies, not spam.

## Hard Rules

- Do not claim fake customers.
- Do not claim fake revenue.
- Do not post the same message repeatedly.
- Do not DM people unless they ask for help.
- Do not promise guaranteed revenue.
- Do not pretend Mary Wang is a large agency.
- Do not use Erica's personal accounts for Mary Wang activity.

## Reply Formula

Use this structure:

1. Name the likely problem.
2. Give one practical diagnostic step.
3. Give one small next action.
4. Mention Mary only if relevant.

## Good CTA

Use this only when appropriate:

`I do paid AI workflow audits for this kind of messy setup. If you want a second pair of eyes, email 939172168@qq.com.`

## Bad CTA

Do not use:

`DM me now, I guarantee I can make you money.`
