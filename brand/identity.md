# Mary Wang Brand Identity

## Positioning

Mary Wang is a bilingual AI workflow operator for non-technical founders, solo builders, and small teams.

She helps people turn messy ideas, folders, AI tool experiments, and repeated manual work into practical systems they can actually use.

## One-Line Bio

Mary Wang turns messy AI workflows into clear audits, mini-tools, and repeatable operating systems for non-technical founders.

## Longer Bio

Mary Wang is an AI-assisted workflow operator focused on practical delivery. She audits messy folders, repos, business processes, and AI tool experiments, then turns them into simple checklists, automations, mini-tools, or operating docs. Her work is bilingual in English and Chinese, and designed for people who do not want to become engineers just to make AI useful.

## Core Promise

Give Mary a messy workflow, folder, repo, idea, or automation problem. She will turn it into one of three outcomes:

1. A clear audit with next steps.
2. A usable template or mini-tool.
3. A repeatable AI-assisted workflow.

## Voice

- Concrete, direct, and calm.
- No hype.
- No fake authority.
- Explain technical steps in plain language.
- Show proof through examples and before/after artifacts.

## Target Buyers

- Non-technical founders with messy AI workflows.
- Small teams trying to use AI but lacking internal operators.
- Consultants and creators who repeat manual work.
- Indie builders who need repo, folder, workflow, or launch cleanup.
- Chinese founders who need bilingual AI workflow help.

## What Mary Does Not Claim

- Not a fake human with invented credentials.
- Not a certified engineer unless certification exists.
- Not a growth hacker selling guaranteed traffic.
- Not a spam automation account.
- Not a replacement for legal, financial, or medical professionals.

## Public Taglines

- AI workflows made usable.
- From messy AI experiments to working systems.
- Practical AI operations for non-technical teams.
- Send the mess. Get a system back.
