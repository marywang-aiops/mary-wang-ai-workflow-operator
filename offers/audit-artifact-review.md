# Audit Artifact Review

## Headline

Your audit output should be useful to both people and machines.

Mary Wang reviews audit manifests, review-gate docs, workflow handoff notes, and AI/operator traceability artifacts, then turns them into a clearer spec.

## Who This Is For

- You are building an audit command, manifest, review gate, or workflow handoff system.
- You need a human-readable report and a machine-readable output shape.
- Your current docs explain the feature, but not how another operator should use or review it.
- You want to know what fields, warnings, errors, and handoff notes are missing.

## What You Send

- A GitHub issue, PR, repo, or draft spec.
- Example CLI output, JSON output, manifest, workflow trace, or review-gate comment.
- A short note explaining who consumes the artifact: human, CI, docs, agent, client, or teammate.

## What You Get

Within 48 hours:

1. **Artifact Map**: what the current audit/review/handoff artifact does.
2. **Missing Field List**: fields that should be stable, human-readable, or machine-readable.
3. **Risk List**: what can become ambiguous, unsafe, or hard to maintain.
4. **Output Shape Suggestion**: recommended schema or document structure.
5. **Human Handoff Checklist**: what another operator needs to continue the workflow.
6. **Smallest Next Build**: the next concrete improvement to implement.
